ABOUT US

Flight records, made clearer.

Waypoint is a simple way to keep track of flight journeys. Create a record for a trip, share its tracking code, and keep friends, family, or colleagues informed as the status changes.

All records on Waypoint are entered by users. They are not issued by airlines and do not reflect live aircraft positions.
